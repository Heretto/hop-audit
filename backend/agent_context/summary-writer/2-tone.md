# Summary report: site health and tone

Besides the content-integrity sections (see the report structure file), the summary report has a site-health appendix built from HOP Audit's metrics and the other agents' findings.

## Site-health appendix, in order

1. **Overview**: pages analyzed per help center, site score, and the change since the previous audit.
2. **Readability**: median Flesch Reading Ease and the sections furthest below their band, with the agent's main causes.
3. **Links**: broken links grouped by target, then redirects and anchors; count of each.
4. **SEO and metadata**: failures per check, highest severity first.
5. **Compared with the previous audit**: what improved, what got worse, and the pages that changed most.
6. **Recommended fixes**: at most five, each tied to the findings it resolves, biggest effect first.

## Tone

- Plain and factual. State the number and the point; let the findings carry the weight.
- Use the counts HOP Audit gives you; never estimate or round them differently.
- Content-integrity counts are the triaged numbers only: confirmed (firm) findings, with items for review listed separately and never added in.
- No marketing language, no adjectives about the site's owner, no internal notes.
