Regulated lending needs governed content

Structured content is critical to controlling risk; without it, the same terms read differently by page, by language, and in every AI answer. A review of 329 of Lumen Financial's public help articles on October 8, 2026 found 63 confirmed issues.

What this means for the business

Regulatory risk
Regulated terms differ from page to page
Credit reporting, eligibility, and dispute rights are stated differently across articles and languages. Regulators judge what the customer was told, and each new market adds rules to keep in step.
From the review
One article says late payments “may lower your credit score.” Another says newer loans “are not reported to credit bureaus at this time.”
AI risk
AI answers inherit every conflict
Companies are bound by what their AI tells customers (Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024), so an assistant answering from these articles needs them to agree.
From the review
“Paid interest is not refundable” in one article. “You may also be eligible for an interest refund” in another.
Cost to serve
Customers act on wrong instructions
Wrong numbers, reversed steps, and conflicting timelines turn self-service into failed payments and support contacts, a load that grows with the customer base.
From the review
The French AutoPay article tells customers to turn AutoPay on to stop automatic payments. A Canadian support number has two digits swapped.
